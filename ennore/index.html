After Cyclone Michaung in December 2023, oil from a refinery spread through Ennore creek in Chennai. The fishers who lost their catch were paid to scoop it out, bucket by bucket. In this small game you do the same, and every bucket brings up one real news story.

What happened

Ennore sits at the northern edge of Chennai, where the Kosasthalaiyar river meets the sea. Its fishing villages share the creek with a refinery, fertiliser plants and coal power stations.

04 dec 2023Cyclone MichaungFloodwater at the CPCL refinery in Manali carries oil into the Buckingham Canal, then the Kosasthalaiyar river and Ennore creek.

About

Average Ennorian is drawn and animated by Hemesh Kanth. It started as a projection piece and is now a game that runs in any phone browser.

The idea comes from Carlo Zanni's Average Shoveler (2004), where you shovel snow on a New York street while the day's news falls around you. Here the snow is oil, and the news is about that oil.

Because it needs nothing to install, a newspaper could print a QR code beside a story and let readers play it.
